09 · Motion

duration.base 150ms · accent → accent.hover
250ms fade + 6px rise duration.relaxed 250ms · delay 60ms — border and text shift at duration.base
  • motion.duration.base — 150ms · color, border, background and underline on a, button
  • motion.duration.relaxed — 250ms · elements that appear rather than change state: tooltip opacity + 6px rise, after a 60ms hover delay
  • motion.ease — cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.2,1) on both durations
  • motion.reduced — all transitions and animations disabled under prefers-reduced-motion; every transition in the system lives inside the no-preference guard

10 · Imagery treatment

Five treatments, one rule behind them: decorative imagery is pulled into the lavender field; imagery that carries information is left alone. Every treatment sits on a surface.strong base and clips with overflow:hidden + isolation:isolate so blend modes stay inside the frame.

All five samples use the same source photograph so the treatments can be compared directly. Blend defaults are exposed as tweaks on the portfolio component (blendModeHome, blendModeContact, blendModeProjects, heroOverlayBlend); multiply is swapped to screen automatically in dark mode.
